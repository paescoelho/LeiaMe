Privacy Policy

Effective July 30, 2026

LeiaMe é um fork do PocketReader, de Charles Neimog, distribuído sob a GPL-3.0.

LeiaMe is an open-source, offline-first PDF and EPUB reader. This policy explains what data LeiaMe processes and what happens when you optionally connect a self-hosted server or Google Drive.

Local use

By default, documents, reading progress, highlights, comments, preferences, and generated caches are stored locally in your browser using browser storage such as IndexedDB and local storage. Local text-to-speech runs on your device. LeiaMe does not include advertising or analytics telemetry.

Google Drive synchronization

If you choose Continue with Google, LeiaMe requests the limited drive.file permission. LeiaMe uses this permission to create a visible LeiaMe folder in your Google Drive and to create, read, update, download, or delete only the documents and synchronization metadata made available to LeiaMe under that permission. Metadata can include reading position, highlights, comments, voice choice, and translation settings.

Google access tokens are kept temporarily in browser memory and browser session storage so that a page reload does not immediately require authorization again. They are not placed in URLs or sent to a LeiaMe developer-operated server. Session credentials expire and are removed when you disconnect. LeiaMe does not sell Google user data or use it for advertising, credit decisions, or user profiling. Google user data is used only to provide the Drive synchronization feature requested by you.

LeiaMe's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Self-hosted server

If you configure and sign in to a self-hosted LeiaMe server, documents and synchronization data are sent to that server. The server operator controls that deployment and is responsible for its security, retention, and privacy practices. Choosing self-host synchronization disables Drive synchronization for the active session.

Reading summary emails

A self-hosted LeiaMe server can use the account email address also used for password recovery to send weekly, monthly, and yearly reading summaries. These summaries are generated from synchronized reward data and can include active reading time, reading-day count, earned growth points, trees completed during the summary period, and the reflections saved when those trees were planted. The server stores the email preference, browser-reported timezone, and a delivery record used to prevent duplicate messages. You can turn all reading summary emails off in LeiaMe Settings.

Translation

Translation is optional. When you request a translation, the selected text can be sent to a translation service or to your configured self-hosted server to produce the result. Do not submit text you do not want processed by the selected translation provider.

Sharing and retention

The LeiaMe project does not sell or rent personal data. Data may be processed by Google when you explicitly use Google authorization, Drive, or translation features, subject to Google's terms and privacy policy. Files stored in Drive remain in your account until you delete them. Disconnecting LeiaMe does not automatically delete the LeiaMe folder or its contents.

Your controls

  • You can use LeiaMe without connecting Google Drive or a self-hosted server.
  • You can remove locally stored documents and highlights from LeiaMe.
  • You can opt out of reading summary emails from LeiaMe Settings.
  • You can delete LeiaMe files directly from your Google Drive.
  • You can revoke LeiaMe's Google access from your Google Account connections.

Changes

This policy may be updated when LeiaMe's behavior changes. The effective date above identifies the current version.

Contact

Questions about privacy can be sent to charlesneimog@outlook.com or raised in the project issue tracker.
